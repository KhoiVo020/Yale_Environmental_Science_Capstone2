# Capstone Prompt 2 discussion draft

Repository: [Yale Environmental Science Capstone](https://github.com/KhoiVo020/Yale_Environmental_Science_Capstone2)

My project examines long-term change and seasonal variation in NOAA's monthly Mauna Loa CO2 record from 1975 to 2025. I standardized column names, created a monthly date index, checked for duplicate or missing months, and selected complete calendar years so later comparisons use consistent coverage. I also converted negative missing-information codes to blank values and flagged the two months with incomplete quality metadata while retaining their published CO2 means. These steps produced 612 monthly records and keep missing metadata from being treated as real measurements; I preserved both the monthly and NOAA-deseasonalized CO2 series so the seasonal pattern and longer-term trend can be examined separately.
